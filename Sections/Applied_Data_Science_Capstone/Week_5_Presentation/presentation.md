The executive summary in the deck states:

- KSC LC-39A had the highest share of successful launches, with more than three quarters of its launches successful.
- The landing rate rises over the years of the study.
- ES-L1, GEO, HEO, and SSO had the best orbit results in the plots, and SO had the worst.
- Boosters that landed on a drone ship in the SQL table sat between 2,000 kg and 6,000 kg of payload, and the FT version is the one called out.
- K-nearest neighbors and the support vector machine are named as the models with the highest and most reliable accuracy.

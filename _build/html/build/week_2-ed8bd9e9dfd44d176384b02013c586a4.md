## SQL results

The saved queries return:

- Launch sites: CCAFS LC-40, VAFB SLC-4E, KSC LC-39A, and CCAFS SLC-40.
- NASA (CRS) payloads sum to 45,596 kg.
- The average payload on boosters whose version starts with `F9 v1.1` is 2,534.7 kg.
- Drone-ship landings with payload from 4,000 kg to 6,000 kg: F9 FT B1022 (4,696 kg), F9 FT B1026 (4,600 kg), F9 FT B1021.2 (5,300 kg), and F9 FT B1031.2 (5,200 kg).
- Mission outcomes: 99 Success, 1 Failure (in flight), and 1 Success (payload status unclear).
- The maximum payload is 15,600 kg, on twelve B5 boosters (B1048.4, B1049.4, B1051.3, B1056.4, B1048.5, B1051.4, B1049.5, B1060.2, B1058.3, B1051.6, B1060.3, and B1049.7).
- Drone-ship landing failures in 2015: 10 January, F9 v1.1 B1012, CCAFS LC-40, and 14 April, F9 v1.1 B1015, CCAFS LC-40.
- The grouped success query lists 12 successful drone-ship landings and 8 successful ground-pad landings. The month and year in that result come from one row of each group.

`Date` is text in day-month-year order. `MIN(Date)` for `Success (ground pad)` returns the string `01-05-2017`. Read as dates, the earliest successful ground-pad landing in `Spacex.csv` is 22 December 2015.

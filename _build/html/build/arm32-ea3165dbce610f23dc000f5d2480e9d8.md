## Introduction

This section is Free Pascal 3.0.4+dfsg-11+rpi1+deb9u1 for arm, dated 2017/07/27. The saved object dump is the integer add. The sum is `add`, and `sxth` narrows it to 16 bits. The addresses of `a`, `b`, and `c` sit in a literal pool after the return.

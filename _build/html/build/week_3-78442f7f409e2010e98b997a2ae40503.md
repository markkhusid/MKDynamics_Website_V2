## Dashboard table

`spacex_launch_dash.csv` has 56 flights. A successful landing is `class` 1. Twenty-four flights are class 1.

| Launch site | Flights | Landings | Landing rate | Share of landings |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| KSC LC-39A | 13 | 10 | 0.769 | 0.417 |
| CCAFS LC-40 | 26 | 7 | 0.269 | 0.292 |
| VAFB SLC-4E | 10 | 4 | 0.400 | 0.167 |
| CCAFS SLC-40 | 7 | 3 | 0.429 | 0.125 |

KSC LC-39A has the most landings and the highest landing rate. The all-sites pie is that share of landings: KSC LC-39A 41.7%, CCAFS LC-40 29.2%, VAFB SLC-4E 16.7%, and CCAFS SLC-40 12.5%. The KSC screenshot is the site pie, 10 successes and 3 failures, which is 76.9% and 23.1%.
